# Merging gnomes

Merge at **any upgrade level**, including completely unupgraded gnomes. Pick an unlocked gnome from the shop and tap a compatible defender on the map: you pay the normal gold price for the new gnome, and they merge at the existing defender’s spot. You can also drag an already placed gnome onto a compatible one, for free.

Alternatively, select a placed gnome, choose **Merge gnomes**, choose another of your compatible defenders, and confirm the preview. The merged gnome occupies the first selected gnome’s spot. Merging is free and lasts for the current game.

The original Sprout/Tumble/Morrow pairs can merge with their missing third gnome. Other recipes are pairs. Every character has at least one listed partner. Three additional experimental recipes are intentionally omitted from this guide and the book. Each form contains at most one of each character; merging does not bypass character unlocks. In co-op, both units must belong to you.

Each component retains its original attack pattern, damage type, range, upgrades, kills, and cooldown. The panel has a tab for each character’s abilities and upgrades. The usual path limits still apply to each component. Selecting a tab shows that component’s range and stats. Targeting is shared by the form.

Morrow adds guardian summoning to all direct attacks in the form, using Morrow’s current guardian upgrades and dispatch limits. Summoned guardians cannot summon more guardians. Existing guardians and queued souls survive merging. Purchased Turbo Tumble triples every component’s attack speed in a form containing Tumble, exactly once. It does not speed up guardian dispatch, guardian melee attacks, or hidden-combination cooldowns.

Merged forms use their own Blender models. Costumes are put aside on those units; owned costumes and equipped collection choices remain saved for new unmerged gnomes. Selling a merged unit removes all its components and guardians and refunds the usual 75% of each component’s original gold purchase cost. Spent upgrade points are not refunded.

Buying directly onto a defender in co-op requires server **0.5.0**, advertising `placementFusionVersion: 1`. The original four forms can also use panel and drag merging on server **0.4.0**, advertising `fusionVersion: 1`. Older servers remain joinable, with merging disabled. Refresh both browsers after updating the server and start a new room.

## Book of Merging

Find the lost book in Moonlit Creek to keep a permanent journal in the menu. Its eleven entries cover merged forms and special combinations. Locked entries give silhouette clues without revealing upgrade recipes. Creating a form or triggering a combo fills its entry, even if you have not found the book yet. Simply placing compatible units or purchasing their upgrades does not count as triggering a combo.

The book and discovered entries are saved to this browser’s collection, alongside your costumes and permanent powers. They survive reloads and new gardens. In co-op the book belongs to your local collection, and combinations you observe in the shared garden count toward it. Finding the book does not unlock a character, grant upgrades, or enable merging on older servers. The book UI works with older servers, but the expanded forms require server 0.5.0 with `fusionCatalogVersion: 2`. The three extra secret recipes never become book entries, even after discovery.

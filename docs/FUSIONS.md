# Merging gnomes

Select a placed Sprout, Tumble, or Morrow, choose **Merge gnomes**, choose another of your compatible defenders, and confirm the preview. The merged gnome occupies the first selected gnome’s spot. Merging is free and lasts for the current game.

| Members | Form |
| --- | --- |
| Sprout + Tumble | Sproutstorm |
| Sprout + Morrow | Gravebloom |
| Tumble + Morrow | Soulstorm |
| All three, in any order | Trinity |

A pair can merge with its missing third gnome. Each form contains at most one of each character; merging does not bypass character unlocks. In co-op, both units must belong to you.

Each component retains its original attack pattern, damage type, range, upgrades, kills, and cooldown. The panel has a tab for each character’s abilities and upgrades. The usual path limits still apply to each component. Selecting a tab shows that component’s range and stats. Targeting is shared by the form.

Morrow adds guardian summoning to all direct attacks in the form, using Morrow’s current guardian upgrades and dispatch limits. Summoned guardians cannot summon more guardians. Existing guardians and queued souls survive merging. Purchased Turbo Tumble triples every component’s attack speed in a form containing Tumble, exactly once. It does not speed up guardian dispatch, guardian melee attacks, or hidden-combination cooldowns.

Merged forms use their own Blender models. Costumes are put aside on those units; owned costumes and equipped collection choices remain saved for new unmerged gnomes. Selling a merged unit removes all its components and guardians and refunds the usual 75% of each component’s original gold purchase cost. Spent upgrade points are not refunded.

Co-op requires server **0.4.0**, advertising `fusionVersion: 1`. Older servers remain joinable, with merging disabled. Refresh both browsers after updating the server and start a new room.

## Book of Merging

Find the lost book in Moonlit Creek to keep a permanent journal in the menu. Its seven entries cover merged forms and special combinations. Locked entries give silhouette clues without revealing upgrade recipes. Creating a form or triggering a combo fills its entry, even if you have not found the book yet. Simply placing compatible units or purchasing their upgrades does not count as triggering a combo.

The book and discovered entries are saved to this browser’s collection, alongside your costumes and permanent powers. They survive reloads and new gardens. In co-op the book belongs to your local collection, and combinations you observe in the shared garden count toward it. Finding the book does not unlock a character, grant upgrades, or enable merging on older servers. The book UI works with the existing 0.4.0 server; no additional Docker update is needed for it.

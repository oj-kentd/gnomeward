Gnomeward server 0.6.1 introduces Skip, the orange-and-blue bouncing-ball gnome.

- Blue balls bounce off garden boundaries until hitting a skeleton. Unupgraded misses fade after 12 seconds.
- Six-Second Surprise adds a six-second explosive fuse; further upgrades improve the blast.
- Multiball upgrades launch two, three, then four balls at different angles.
- Merge Tumble and Morrow into Soulstorm, then add Skip to create Soulbounce. Each member keeps its abilities and upgrades.
- Merge Aster + Morel into Sporewatch, retaining long-range shots and poison mushrooms.
- Defeated Soulbounce ghosts release one bouncing ball with Skip’s upgrades, without recursive ghost summoning.
- Includes new Blender models and portraits, authoritative co-op combat, and older-server capability checks.
- Existing saves and secret recipes are preserved.

Download the image and SHA256SUMS, verify the checksum, then load the image with Docker. Set the Unraid container Repository to `gnomeward-server:0.6.1` and Apply. Keep existing data, ports, and environment settings. Finish active games before restarting.
